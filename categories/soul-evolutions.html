Blood Spells

Blood Wave
Shoot out a wave of acidic blood in the direction you're facing.

Blood Surge
Blood surges through your veins, increasing your strength and speed.

Frost Spells

Frost Wave
Shoot out a wave of icy energy in the direction you're facing.

Frost Bloom
Envelop yourself in a barrier of ice, exploding outwards with icy energy.

Major Arcana

0. The Fool
The first attack the player performs every 10 seconds will apply a random stackable positive potion effect onto them.
Each attack done during the 10 second cooldown will decrease the remaining cooldown time by 0.25 seconds.

I. The Magician
All mana-consuming action performed have an increased potency (the strength of an effect/damage of a spell)
of 80%, but require and extra 55% longer cast-time.

II. The High Priestess

III. The Empress

IV. The Emperor

V. The Hierophant
Any healing action capable of healing other players will heal an additional 0.5% per 2%
of the target player’s hp missing when healing any player other than the caster.

VI. The Lovers
Link souls with another player. When two players are soul linked, damage taken is split between both players,
and the player who initiated the soul link receives 20% of both attack power and defence of the other linked player’s.
If either player dies, the other will as well, and the soul link ability will enter a long state of cooldown.<
Tarot card cannot be switched while on cooldown.

XIII. Death
“Kill” a player. Upon fullfiling certain conditions, mark an entity with a death curse.
If the victim fails to complete their provided task/conditions,
they gradually become weaker and weaker, until they die.

XIV. Temperance

XV. The Devil

XVI. The Tower
Gain immunity to all displacement (knock back/abilities that force movement).
Increase the armor and armor toughness stats by 20% and 10%, and 40% and 20% while standing still.
Player can switch between defensive states, providing protection from either magic, blunt or piercing damage.
